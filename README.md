## 🚀 What is BeamDrop?

**BeamDrop** is a free, open-source peer-to-peer file transfer application designed to provide an Apple AirDrop-like experience across **Windows** and **Android**.

Unlike cloud drives (Google Drive, Dropbox, WeTransfer) or Bluetooth, BeamDrop operates **100% offline** over your local Wi-Fi or mobile hotspot. It transfers photos, 4K videos, zip files, and entire folders at physical network link speed (**80–120+ MB/s**) with **zero cloud uploads**, **zero data quota consumed**, and **zero tracking**.

## 📱 How to Install on Android

1. Download [**BeamDrop-Android.apk**](https://beamdrop.pritamjoardar.com/downloads/BeamDrop-Android.apk) directly on your Android smartphone or tablet.
2. Open your device's notification tray or **Files / Downloads** app and tap the downloaded `.apk`.
3. If Android prompts *"For your security, your phone is not allowed to install unknown apps from this source"*:
   - Tap **Settings** in the popup.
   - Toggle **Allow from this source** to ON.
   - Return and tap **Install**.
4. Open BeamDrop and allow local network permissions when requested.

## 💻 How to Use BeamDrop

### Step 1: Connect Both Devices to the Same Local Network
Ensure your Windows PC and Android phone are connected to:
- The same home or office **Wi-Fi router**, **OR**
- Your phone's **Mobile Hotspot** (Internet/data can be turned OFF; BeamDrop only needs local Wi-Fi connectivity).

### Step 2: Open BeamDrop
Launch BeamDrop on your PC and your phone. The animated **Radar View** will automatically discover nearby devices in less than a second via local UDP beaconing.

### Step 3: Send Files & Folders
- **From Windows:** Drag and drop files or folders directly onto the radar or onto the avatar of your phone.
- **From Android:** Tap the **Send Files** button in the header, choose your media or documents, and select your PC.

### Step 4: Accept & Stream
A permission card will appear on the receiving device showing the sender's name and file manifest. Tap **Accept** to begin the high-speed stream! Once finished, tap **Open Folder** to view your received files instantly in your system Downloads folder.

## ⚡ Speed & Privacy Comparison

| Metric | BeamDrop Local P2P | Cloud Drives (Drive, WeTransfer) | Bluetooth 5.0 |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Transfer Speed** | **80 – 120+ MB/s** | Dependent on Internet (~5–15 MB/s) | ~2 MB/s |
| **Internet / Cellular Data** | **0 MB (Zero)** | 2x File Size (Upload + Download) | 0 MB |
| **Cloud Storage Limits** | **Unlimited** | Limited by cloud quota | N/A |
| **Privacy & Security** | **100% Local LAN (E2E SHA-256)** | Third-party servers store your files | Local |
